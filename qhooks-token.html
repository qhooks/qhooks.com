The QHOOKS plan, in the open.

Where every token goes.

49.06% went into the pool at launch. 20.94% went to the opening buy by early supporters. The other 30% is split into five slices, each in its own on-chain lock with a public schedule. Before any locked tokens can be withdrawn, the lock posts a public notice on chain, and nothing moves for 7 days.

About the operations & hiring treasury (12%, 120M QHOOKS). Locked at launch. Nothing unlocks for the first month, then it releases a little each day over the next 23 months to the QHooks Safe. It funds the team as QHooks grows: hiring, salaries, contributors and building. Every release is on chain for anyone to check, with a 7-day on-chain notice before each one.
About the drops. Drops run automatically once the distributor is live; until then unlocked tokens stay in the QHooks Safe.

Claim drops.

Drops for holders and stakers, plus callout rewards, for the connected wallet. Claim them all in one transaction per chain. Every list is public, and each one waits 24 hours on chain before it can be claimed.

The 2% tax, line by line.

Every QHOOKS buy and sell pays 2%. The split is written into the launch’s contracts and can’t be changed after launch.

0.75%0.75%0.25%0.25%
  • QHooks platform, paid in WETH 0.75%
  • Buyback and burn, every 15 minutes 0.75%
  • Stakers, streamed daily 0.25%
  • Referrals, from the QHOOKS tax 0.25%
Platform buybacks

25% of platform revenue on Robinhood Chain

Launch fees, pool protocol fees and the terminal’s platform share on Robinhood Chain feed an on-chain flywheel that buys QHOOKS back. Half of what it buys is burned and half goes to the treasury Safe.

Builder fund buys

10% of platform revenue, every month

Each month the Safe spends 10% of platform revenue buying QHOOKS for the builder fund. This is a manual Safe transaction, recorded publicly.

Buybacks & burns, live.

Read from the chain every 20 seconds: what the tax has collected for buybacks, when the next run is allowed, and every QHOOKS burned so far.

What holding QHOOKS does.

  • Stake

    A share of the tax

    Stakers share the 0.25% stakers’ cut, paid in WETH and QHOOKS and streamed daily. You can unstake at any time.

  • Cashback

    30% back on EVM trades

    40% on Robinhood Chain if you hold 100K QHOOKS.

    Trade QHOOKS
  • Curation

    A say on new modules

    Holders signal on modules submitted to the builder marketplace, weighted by balance. It’s advisory: the Safe still makes the final listing call. Built, and switched on at launch.

  • Builders

    How builders get paid

    Bounties, grants and monthly builder payments are paid in QHOOKS from the builder fund, and only from what has already unlocked.

    • All locks created, with their addresses published on this page. Live

Plainly.

  • Nothing is guaranteed. QHOOKS is a utility token for the QHooks ecosystem. This page is a plan, not a promise or an offer, and plans can change. If they do, we’ll update this page and say what changed.
  • Staking payouts depend on volume. Stakers share a cut of the trading tax. With little trading there is little to share. There is no fixed rate.
  • Unlocks add supply. Locked slices unlock on the schedule above. Every withdrawal needs a public 7-day notice, but unlocked tokens can still be sold.
  • Buybacks depend on revenue. The flywheel and the monthly builder-fund buys only spend what the platform actually earns, and the flywheel runs through a keeper.
  • One chain. QHOOKS lives on Robinhood Chain. Moving between chains isn’t part of this plan.
  • Smart contract risk. The contracts are tested and reviewed, but no code is free of risk. An independent security review is on the roadmap.
