KEEP YOUR SETUP PORTABLE

Moving clients with NOMAD?

Keep an existing QHooks HTTP entry when you move a setup. NOMAD is an independent portability tool; this recipe adds no automatic starter preset.

  1. Choose the QHooks entry you already use and review NOMAD's destination preview.
  2. Preserve https://qhooks.com/mcp?ref=nomad and the entry's enabled or paused state.
  3. Check destination tool permissions. Some adapters cannot carry source tool filters; a warning does not enforce a restriction.
  4. Reconnect your signing wallet separately. Keys, balances, wallet permissions and transaction consent do not move with an MCP URL.

ATTRIBUTION, WITHOUT CONFUSION

A source tag isn't a fee referrer.

?ref=nomad labels aggregate setup traffic. For eligible QHooks spot trades, pass the agent's public EVM wallet explicitly as referrer in the preparation arguments. Bring traders, get 20% of their fee.

The preparation must return that same referrer. Self-referral and linked-wallet exclusions apply. Referral eligibility follows the live QHooks rules; this does not promise fees from partner protocols.
