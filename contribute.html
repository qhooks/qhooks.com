How it works

  1. Anyone with the round address can put in. Send ETH to it from your own wallet, or use the button here. It sits in the round contract, not in anyone's wallet.
  2. Your QHOOKS and any refund go back to the address you sent from. Don't send from an exchange or from a multisig or other contract wallet: you couldn't claim.
  3. Until the round closes you can take your ETH back in full.
  4. At launch, the round's ETH (up to 0.5 ETH) is the first buy of QHOOKS, inside the same transaction that creates the token and its pool. No one can trade before it.
  5. The group buy is 0.5 ETH, about 20% of the supply. If more than 0.5 ETH comes in, everyone is scaled down by the same fraction and the rest of their ETH comes back when they claim.
  6. You get the QHOOKS it bought in proportion to your ETH. Nobody's share is fixed in advance.
  7. Your QHOOKS are claimable straight after launch and are not locked. After launch, come back here and tap Claim — with the same wallet you sent from.
  8. If the round is called off, closes below its minimum, or the launch does not happen by the deadline shown above, everyone takes their full ETH back.

Contributing is a risk. QHOOKS can trade below what the group paid, and the 2% token tax applies to trades after launch. This page reads the round contract directly; it holds no funds and keeps no records. Not investment advice.
