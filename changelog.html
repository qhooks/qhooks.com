Changelog

What’s new on QHooks

Everything we shipped, newest first.

November 4, 2026

Build

Every Lab hook can go to a public chain

TWAP Oracle, Price Bands, Volume Epochs, Directional Fees, Circuit Recovery and Revenue Split now deploy from the Lab to Base, Robinhood Chain, Ethereum, Arbitrum One and BNB Chain, like Creator Revenue and Dynamic Fees: build for the chain, approve each transaction in your own wallet, and the Lab checks the deployed code against the build byte for byte.

Every policy change on a governed hook needs your wallet and your post-quantum key, the same two keys token launches use. Move a price band, open the next volume epoch, schedule new directional fees or resume a tripped circuit from the hook's share page.

Revenue Split pays two payees into two separate vaults; each withdraws their own share with their own wallet and their own post-quantum key, which payee 2 creates in their own browser.

The TWAP Oracle has no owner and no keys: deploy it, and any contract can read the pool's time-weighted price.

Trade

Know who made a token before you buy

Creators can link their X account to their launch: their wallet signs a short message and they post the code it gives them. Verified launches show the creator's handle on the token page, in the terminal list and on share cards.

A launch that reuses the ticker or name of an earlier verified launch is labelled as a copy and listed below the rest. Nothing is blocked; you can still buy and sell it.

Every token page now shows a pre-buy check from RepoProof next to the launch's own facts, read from its contracts: blocks switched on, what a buy and a sell pay right now, permanent liquidity, team tokens, creator keys and modules.

Grow

Creator profiles and a community hub

Every wallet that launched on QHooks has a public profile showing its launches, their pools and modules. A new Builders program page explains how to propose modules, launch blocks, hooks and tools, and a Community page gathers our X account, the Telegram bot, builder docs and how to install the app.

Integrate

Public API and SDK for developers

A read-only public API lists every launch, its pools, trades, holders and candles, and quotes a trade through the pool's own rules. A small JavaScript SDK builds swaps through Uniswap's Universal Router or the QHooks fee router. Routers and aggregators can see, per pool, when its launch rules allow outside routing.

Grow

My QHooks and creator dashboards

One page for your wallet across every chain: the launches you created and the income waiting for them, your stakes, raffle entries, vesting streams, cashback and referral rewards, modules you built and your holdings, each with its claim button.

Each launch has a creator dashboard with its payouts, holders, traders and every tool its creator uses.

Build

Deploy Lab hooks to public chains

The Creator Revenue and Dynamic Fees templates now deploy from the Lab to Base, Robinhood Chain, Ethereum, Arbitrum One and BNB Chain, from your own wallet. The Lab checks the deployed code byte for byte against the build and gives you a share page anyone can re-check.

Build

Vesting streams and a TWAP oracle template

Any payee in a launch's revenue split can be paid over 30, 90 or 180 days or a year instead of at once. The stream pays only its beneficiary and anyone can push payouts.

The Lab has a new template: a TWAP price oracle hook for one pool.

Build

Builder marketplace

Outside builders can now write revenue modules that launches add to their revenue split, and earn up to 10% of what each module receives. QHooks reviews every module before it is offered in Studio. The first two are holder staking and a daily no-loss holder raffle.

Site

A new home for the ecosystem

New navigation on every page, organised as Build, Launch, Trade, Grow, Protect and Integrate, and a new homepage that starts with launching a token on your own hooks.

Grow

Holder staking

A launch can share part of its income with holders who stake. Rewards stream over a day, and the terminal has a Staking tab to stake, unstake and claim.

Trade

Trade any token from the terminal

Search any token on Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum and BNB Chain, or on Solana, and trade it from the terminal. Outside tokens show a safety scan before you trade, and a high-risk result asks you to confirm.

Install QHooks, or open it in Telegram

Add QHooks to your phone's home screen like an app, or open the terminal inside Telegram from the QHooks bot.

Trade

Terminal rewards and polish

A Rewards button in the terminal shows the cashback and referral rewards you can claim on every chain. Token rows, charts for young launches, the stats bar and the trade ticket are cleaner, on desktop and on phones.

Launch

Lower launch fee

Launching a token now costs about 45 cents, paid in Studio.

Launch

Share links with preview cards

Every launch has a short share link that shows a preview card on X and in chat apps and opens the launch in the terminal.

Quick launch

Launch a token in a few steps: pick a chain and a style (fair launch, degen, community, burn engine or market hours), name it and confirm. Studio does the rest in order.

Protect

Sign in with email, Google or any phone wallet

Connect with email or Google (no extension needed), MetaMask Mobile, any phone wallet through WalletConnect, or a browser wallet. The wallet window opens instantly and stays connected when a trade switches networks.

Trade

Telegram alerts

Follow a launch from its page and get Telegram alerts for its key events. The terminal also got a phone pass with a jump bar.

Protect

Creator key rotation and recovery

Creators can replace their keys, and recover from a lost wallet or a lost post-quantum key. A recovery takes 90 days and the other key can cancel it; every launch page shows when one is under way.

Launch

Liquidity is permanent

Launch liquidity can never be withdrawn, by anyone, and graduation moves all of it to the new pool at the current price. The platform's share of pool fees is paid inside every swap. Each launch page has a Launch terms card that reads these rules from its contracts.

Protect

Platform fees go to a multi-signature wallet

Every platform fee is paid to a QHooks multi-signature wallet that needs two signers to move funds.

Trade

Terminal fees with cashback

Terminal trades carry a 1% fee, and part of it comes back to you as cashback you can claim. Launching charges a small flat fee.

Trade

Take-profit, stop-loss and wallet tracking

Set take-profit and stop-loss orders on any launch, track wallets with alerts when they trade, copy a tracked wallet's buy in one click, and see each launch's top traders.

Trade

The trading terminal

Every QHooks launch across our chains in one place: a live feed, candle charts, the trade tape, holders, your positions and a trade ticket that quotes through each pool and its rules. Keyboard shortcuts included.

Launch

Opening auctions, second pools and team tokens

A launch can open with a short auction where everyone gets the same price, add a token/ETH pool next to its main pool, and lock, burn or keep its team tokens right after launch.

Trade

Buy with ETH, limit orders and team locks

Buy any launch with ETH, place limit orders, and see team tokens locked on a public schedule with a sell notice before any of them can move.

Launching on Arc is paused until a live launch there proves pool funding works. The contracts still support it.
