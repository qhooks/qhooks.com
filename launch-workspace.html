    Manage your launch
    Claim fees, graduate or withdraw. Every protected action needs both keys.
    
  
    
   
    
    
    Zero accepts any nonnegative payout. Set minimum amounts to limit unfavorable changes. Values use whole-token units.
   
    
   
    Graduation moves the whole position into the destination pool at the current price. Nothing is paid out: a sliver left over (0.25%, the margin for price movement while you sign) is burned. The transaction reverts if the price moves more than about 1% before it lands.
    
   
    Activation waits through your original notice period plus a 15-minute signing allowance. Announcing does not apply the policy automatically.

    How launch protection works
    SLH-DSA-SHA2-128s and your EVM wallet jointly authorize pool initialization, graduation-policy changes, fee claims, migration and destination withdrawals. The token remains a standard ERC20. Ordinary transfers, swaps, chain consensus and assets outside these contracts are not made quantum-protected.
    There is no upfront platform charge. You fund initial liquidity and pay network fees for each transaction. Trading uses your configured LP fee plus the separately disclosed protocol surcharge. Liquidity values can fall; no yield or return is guaranteed.
    Contracts are not independently audited. Keep your encrypted key backup, its passphrase and the separate public launch-progress file. Do not send tokens directly to contract addresses to initialize liquidity.
