Public journals contain configuration, public signatures and transaction progress—not your private key, encrypted backup or passphrase. Importing a journal can check or recover a transaction; it cannot sign or resend one.

  

 

 
Two keys. One deliberate move.
Bootstrap, policy changes, migration, fee claims and destination exits use wallet plus SLH-DSA-SHA2-128s authorization. Token allowances use your ordinary wallet; ordinary token transfers are not quantum-protected. Fees here mean accrued trading fees, not guaranteed yield.
Migration stays within the fixed token pair and manager on the same EVM chain. There is no bridge. The local RPC must support the read-only state overrides used to check exact hook runtime code. Service fees are off.
