Privacy policy

What QHooks stores, who processes it, and how to delete it. QHooks runs qhooks.com. Questions go to team@qhooks.com.

Last updated 12 Nov 2026

Your keys and your funds

QHooks never holds your keys or funds. Trades and launches are signed in your own wallet.

Email, Google and X sign-in and embedded wallets are provided by Privy, which holds those wallet keys under its own terms.

What we store

Only when you use these features:

  • public profile fields you choose: name, bio, picture, links and handle;
  • accounts you link and verify (X, Discord, Farcaster), which you can hide or unlink;
  • your follows, calls, posts and thanks;
  • token profiles creators publish: icon, banner and links;
  • your wallet address or addresses;
  • trade-sharing settings, if you opt in;
  • web push subscriptions, if you turn alerts on;
  • your Telegram user id, if you link @qhooks_bot.

What is public

Wallet addresses and on-chain activity are public on the blockchain. Profiles, calls and posts are public.

Who processes requests

These service providers handle requests for us:

  • Cloudflare, for hosting. Request logs are sampled.
  • Privy, for sign-in.
  • Blockchain RPC providers, including Alchemy and public RPCs, for balances and transactions.
  • DexScreener, for market data.
  • 0x, Jupiter, Meteora and Hyperliquid, for the quotes and trades you request.
  • Telegram, if you use the bot.
  • Your browser’s push service, if you turn alerts on.

No selling, no trackers

We don’t sell personal data, and we use no advertising or analytics trackers.

Deleting your data

To delete your profile data, email team@qhooks.com. On-chain data can’t be deleted.

Age

QHooks is not for people under 18.

Contact

QHooks, team@qhooks.com. See also the terms of use.
