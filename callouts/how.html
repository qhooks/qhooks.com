How callout points work

Every day, 10% of the terminal’s trading fees on Robinhood Chain goes to callers. It is shared by points, and points come from the gains of people who bought because of your call.

Points

A call
A token you hold, posted with your wallet’s signature. Anyone can call.
Points
1 point = 1 cent made by people who bought because of the call within 24 hours, marked at the end of the next day (UTC). A losing call scores zero.
Who counts
Someone who bought through the call’s Buy button or call link while signed in, or a wallet that followed you at least a day before buying. The wallet must be at least a week old on QHooks and put in at least $10.
Never counts
Your own wallets, wallets you funded, calls on a token you launched, and your own gain on the token.

Adjustments

Still holding
1.25× when you still hold the token you called at scoring time, checked on chain.
Calls a day
Your first 3 calls a day count fully. Later calls count less.
Market cap
Under $100K at the call counts less. Over $1M counts 1.5×, over $10M counts 2×.
Daily limit
At most 100,000 points per caller per day.

The day’s pool

Share
Your points ÷ everyone’s points that day. When 4 or more callers score, no one takes more than 25% and the rest is shared again by points. With fewer, it is straight by points, so a lone caller gets the whole day.
Payout
WETH on Robinhood Chain with the weekly drop. Claim it in My QHooks.
Gain on a stake
Call cards show the caller’s stake and the gain on it. That is information only: it is not a payout and not points.
