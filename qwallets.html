My QHooks
QWallet / Cross-EVM

A second key.
Your control.

Withdrawals take your wallet and a post-quantum signature. Base, Robinhood, Ethereum, Arbitrum, BNB or Arc.

Connect → Set up → Back up your quantum key → Enroll → Deposit.

Choose your chain

Choose your network in your wallet before connecting. No automatic chain switching.

The native asset is always included. Add up to 16 token contracts. This list cannot change after deployment. On Arc, use native USDC, not its ERC20 alias.

Set up your QWallet

Deploy the verifier, then your custody contract. Nothing is deposited during setup.

Save or recover setup

Keep this public progress file when moving between devices or domains. It does not contain your keys.

Your quantum key

Your quantum key is generated locally and never shared with QHooks. An encrypted backup is required to protect your assets. No seed or wallet private key is ever requested.

Create a key, enter your passphrase and download its encrypted backup. Then choose the downloaded JSON file and re-enter the same passphrase to verify it.

Use at least 16 characters. Keep the passphrase separate from your backup.

If you lose your backup or passphrase, QHooks cannot reset it. Protection applies to assets inside QWallet.

How QWallet protection works
QWallet uses SLH-DSA-SHA2-128s post-quantum signatures alongside your connected EVM wallet. Protection applies to withdrawals from this custody contract, not your ordinary wallet, token transfers, chain consensus or assets in bridge transit. QHooks cannot reset either key.
Contracts are not independently audited. Tokens can have issuer controls, upgrades, transfer fees or blocklists; unsupported token behavior can cause a transaction to revert. QWallet does not provide yield. Use the deposit action, not a direct transfer to the contract.
Reviews expire after two minutes. Account or network changes lock your quantum key and invalidate approvals. Keep both your encrypted backup and public setup progress before moving this workspace to another site.
