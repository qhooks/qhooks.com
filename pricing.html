Frequently asked questions

What costs nothing upfront?

Standard hooks and supported-template Lab builds have no platform fee. Downloads and repeat downloads are free. A token launch has a flat fee of about $0.45 in the chain’s coin, shown with gas and liquidity in Create’s launch review.

What fees apply to a launch pool?

Your configured creator LP rate applies to supported-pool swaps. The protocol rate is one fifth of that rate, rounded down to whole parts per million and added separately. For example, 1% creator LP plus 0.2% protocol is 1.2% nominal. These rates are not a swap quote and do not apply to ordinary transfers or other venues. The protocol part is paid to the platform automatically in every trade.

What else do I fund?

You pay network gas and choose the token and quote-asset liquidity for launch. Those amounts are shown separately at review. Opting into ML-DSA also requires explicit review of its additional network cost.

Can my platform use the API and hooks?

The public read API and SDK are free to use. Discover hooks, read deployment evidence and integrate QHooks pools into your app. Supported Lab builds have no upfront platform fee; you fund deployment gas. Build on QHooks →

Solana launches

A fixed one-billion-supply coin. Immutable metadata. No team allocation. Launch for about $0.45 in SOL, plus rent and network costs.

Trading fee: 1.2% per trade. You 0.8% · QHooks 0.16% · Meteora 0.24% of each trade.

Anti-snipe: starts at 50% and falls to 1.2% over the first 60 seconds. Your own first buy pays it too.

At 85 SOL the coin moves to a Meteora DAMM v2 pool at the curve’s last price. Liquidity is locked forever. Nobody can withdraw it.

Traders get 30% of the QHooks share back; referrers get 20%. Jupiter routes can add their own integrator fee.
