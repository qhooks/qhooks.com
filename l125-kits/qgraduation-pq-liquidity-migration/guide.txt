QGraduation | QHooks
====================

Launch a token with post-quantum-controlled liquidity and a graduation policy you choose.

Protected action: Controller-owned liquidity actions and graduation policy changes require wallet and post-quantum approval.
Profile: SLH-DSA

Choose Base, Robinhood Chain, Ethereum, Arbitrum One, BNB Chain or Arc in the builder. The downloadable Base example is public configuration only; it is not a key backup or deployed contract. Network and liquidity costs are shown in the launch flow. Net quote inflow is not market cap. Protection covers the controller-managed position, not arbitrary external pools, wallet transfers or cross-chain transit.

GET STARTED
1. Open https://qhooks.com/graduation-studio.html
2. Import launch-recipe.json, choose your chain and replace the example token settings.
3. Preview and continue into launch. Connect your wallet only when ready.
4. Create your private quantum key and verify its encrypted backup. Never share it.
5. Review network fees and approve each transaction separately.

This public recipe contains no keys, signatures, deployed addresses or spending authority. Its example uses Base and is not evidence of a deployment. You can change the chain in the builder. It must never be used as a key backup.
