ML-DSA treasury + Creator Revenue (optional)Create or resume a treasury launch with separate profile and transaction-cost consent.

This route puts your initial fixed token supply in a QWallet treasury and protects treasury actions and fee claims. It is a different workflow from QGraduation. Opening this panel does not select ML-DSA, connect a wallet, or authorize a transaction.

1. Connect your wallet

Use a local EVM node with a block gas limit of at least 80 million and a funded disposable account. Current key registration uses about 39.5 million gas per treasury or vault; this flow is not a public-chain deployment.

Opt in to ML-DSA-65

This treasury workflow uses the Category 3 ML-DSA-65 profile, with a separate cost review before every transaction. Selecting it here does not convert a QGraduation setup, QPool account, or their keys.

Nothing is charged by selecting this option. You approve each transaction and its final fee in your wallet.

2. Make the token yours

With 18 decimals, 1 token is 1,000,000,000,000,000,000 smallest units. The local liquidity demonstration requires at least 2,000,000 tokens at 18 decimals.

The token is a conventional ERC20. Your treasury actions and fee claims require both wallet and post-quantum signatures. The included quote token and pool router are local test fixtures.

Resume a launch

Public progress is saved in this browser. Resume rechecks the actual transaction receipts. Your quantum key and backup passphrase are never saved here or sent to the server.
