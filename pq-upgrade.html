My QHooks

Post-quantum

Upgrade your launch to post-quantum

A one-tap launch has no owner, no pause, no upgrade and no mint. Its only role is the creator's: choosing where the launch's LP fees go, and handing that role on. Upgrading puts that role behind two keys: your wallet and your QHooks quantum key.

Loading…

1. Your quantum key

One quantum key for your QHooks account (SLH-DSA, a hash-based signature standard, NIST FIPS 205). It is made and kept encrypted in your browser and opens with your QHooks sign-in, Face ID / Touch ID, or a passphrase. QHooks never sees it.

2. Your launches

Loading…

What is enforced, and where

  • Once upgraded, changing where LP fees go, handing the role on or locking it needs your wallet and a quantum-key signature, checked on chain by the contract below. Either one alone is refused.
  • Fees always keep flowing to the payout address. Nothing in the contract can stop them, even if both keys are lost.
  • Lost a key? Either key alone can start a public recovery. It completes after 30 days unless the other key cancels it. Watch this page: a pending recovery shows here.
  • Signatures are tied to the chain, the launch, the exact action, a counter and a 15-minute window, so they cannot be reused.
  • QHooks Studio launches already require both keys for every creator action. One-tap launches that are not upgraded keep a wallet-only creator role.
