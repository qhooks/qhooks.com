My QHooks

Founding Launchers

The first 50 launches on QHooks earn more

Launch a token on QHooks, one-tap or Studio, on any chain. The first 50 launches from this page's start time are Founding Launchers. One per creator wallet.

Refreshing…

What founding launches get

  1. You earn on your token's volume for 90 days. On every trade of your token that comes without a referral link, you get the 20% referral share of the QHooks fee, the same share a referrer would get. Traders who arrive through someone else's link stay with that referrer.
  2. 50,000 QHOOKS when your token takes off. Reach 100 holders and $5,000 of trading volume through QHooks within 14 days of launch. The grant vests over 30 days and you claim it as it vests. Your own trades don't count toward the volume, and each trader counts for at most a quarter of it. Grants come from a 2.5M QHOOKS budget, in the order milestones are reached.
  3. Your launch is pinned. Founding launches get their own row in the terminal's Launches list and are shared from the QHooks accounts.
  4. Trust checks come with it. Each founding launch gets a RepoProof check, a prompt to verify the launch with your X account, and a link to your builder record where one exists.
  5. Your traders get 50% cashback for the first 7 days. Traders already get 30% of the fee back. For your token's first 7 days, they get another 20%.

How it's paid

The volume share and the extra cashback are paid in WETH on Robinhood Chain through the weekly QHooks drop list, which you claim from My QHooks with the same wallet. Fees taken in ETH or WETH count one to one. Fees in USDC, USDG, BNB or HYPE count at that day's closing price. Fees taken in stock tokens are not counted. This is the same on every chain: Robinhood Chain, Base, Arbitrum, BNB Chain, Ethereum and Elysium.

The QHOOKS grant is a QGrants grant on Robinhood Chain from the QHooks Safe, sent in weekly batches. The Safe can take back what hasn't vested if a milestone turns out to be faked.

QHooks' own wallets and test launches are not counted.
