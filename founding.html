Can I enter?

Yes. Any QHooks launch takes a spot, one per creator wallet, until the 50 are gone. No QHOOKS holding is needed. Launching on Solana? Rewards go to an EVM wallet. If you launched signed in to QHooks with an EVM wallet on your account, it links by itself. If not, link one below before the first weekly drop after your launch: one signature from it and one from your Solana launch wallet, no transaction.

Qualifying chains

Robinhood Chain, Base, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, Elysium, Ethereum (opening soon) and Solana (opening soon).

What founding launches get

  1. You earn on your token's volume for 90 days. On every trade of your token that comes without a referral link, you get the 20% referral share of the QHooks fee, the same share a referrer would get. Traders who arrive through someone else's link stay with that referrer. Solana launches do not earn this share yet.
  2. 50,000 QHOOKS when your token takes off. Reach 100 holders holding at least $10 worth each, and $5,000 of real trading volume through QHooks, within 14 days of launch. The grant vests over 30 days and you claim it as it vests. Your own trades, wallets you sent the token to, and wash trades don't count, and each trader counts for at most a quarter of the volume. Grants come from a 2.5M QHOOKS budget, in the order milestones are reached.
  3. Your launch is pinned. Founding launches get their own row in the terminal's Launches list and are shared from the QHooks accounts.
  4. Trust checks come with it. Each founding launch gets a RepoProof check, a prompt to verify the launch with your X account, and a link to your builder record where one exists.
  5. Your traders get 50% cashback for the first 7 days. Traders already get 30% of the fee back. For your token's first 7 days, they get another 20%. The cashback boost does not apply to Solana traders.
  6. $1,000 to the first to $150,000. The first founding token to hold a $150,000 market cap for 24 hours straight wins $1,000, paid to its creator in WETH on Robinhood Chain. For all 24 hours the market cap must also be at least 10 times the token's starting market cap, so a high starting price can't win it. At the end it needs 100 or more holders holding $10 or more each, and $25,000 or more of real trading volume since launch. The creator's wallets don't count as holders, and your own and wash trades don't count as volume. Market cap is the on-chain price times total supply, checked every 5 minutes; one dip restarts the 24 hours. On Solana the price is the latest trade, and 30 minutes with no trade also restarts the 24 hours.

How it's paid

The volume share and the extra cashback are paid in WETH on Robinhood Chain through the weekly QHooks drop list, which you claim from My QHooks with the same wallet. Fees taken in ETH or WETH count one to one. Fees in USDC, USDG, BNB or HYPE count at that day's closing price. Fees taken in stock tokens are not counted. Solana grants and the bounty are paid to the linked EVM reward wallet on Robinhood Chain.

The QHOOKS grant is a QGrants grant on Robinhood Chain from the QHooks Safe, sent in weekly batches. The Safe can take back what hasn't vested if a milestone turns out to be faked.

QHooks' own wallets and test launches are not counted.

We may withhold rewards for wash trading, sybil holders or other manipulation. On Solana, wallets funded from the same source count as one holder.
