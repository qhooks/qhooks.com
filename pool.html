My QHooks

Pools / custody and launch

Protect assets. Build liquidity.

Choose a chain, then open the path you need. QWallet holds the chain’s native asset and selected ERC20 balances with two-key withdrawal protection. The launch workspace prepares token creation and launch liquidity.

Your connected EVM wallet and separate SLH-DSA-SHA2-128s key protect QWallet withdrawals.

Selected chain

Open protected custodyOpen full launch liquidity workflow

QWallet lets you set an immutable supported-token list, deposit assets and prepare protected withdrawals. The separate launch workspace handles token creation and launch liquidity. Each destination performs its own wallet connection and transaction review.

What protected custody covers

Custody does not earn yield or create an AMM position. Swaps and LP position management are separate capabilities.
