How blocks work

  1. PickChoose blocks in Studio and tune each one. A preview shows what a buy and a sell will pay, minute by minute.
  2. LaunchYour blocks deploy with your pool. Their settings are written once and have no owner function.
  3. TradeEvery swap runs through them. Charges sit in the blocks contract until anyone releases them to your payees.

Built in

Every launch comes with these.

Tools on every Trade page

They work with any QHooks launch, old or new. Each runs on one public contract per chain with no owner, at the same address on every chain.

Buy with ETH

Pay in ETH or BNB on a USDC or WETH pool. Trade compares Uniswap’s ETH / USDC pools, takes the best route and buys in one transaction. The quote runs through the pool’s blocks, so every charge is included.

Limit orders

Set a price to buy the dip or sell into strength. Your funds wait in the orders contract, and anyone can fill the order once the market reaches your price, earning the tip you set. You never get less than your price after fees. Cancel any time.

Second pool

The creator can open a token / ETH market next to the launch pool. It runs the launch’s own blocks: the same charges, guards and burns, paid to the same payees, so there is no cheaper side door. Its liquidity can never be withdrawn; the creator collects the LP fees.

Team locks

Lock team tokens with a cliff and a drip. Every sale needs a public on-chain notice first, which shows on the Trade page before it can run. Locks can be extended but never shortened or cancelled.

Vesting streams

Pay the creator’s share, or a treasury’s, through a vesting stream instead of straight to a wallet. Each payout reaches the wallet evenly over 30 days to a year, and nobody can change who it pays or how fast. Pick it on any payee in Studio; the Trade page shows what is vesting and when it is done.

Read any pool’s blocks.

Each launch’s blocks are a public contract. Its settings, pots, points and the charge a trade will pay right now are readable by anyone, from any wallet or explorer. The Trade page shows them before you swap.
