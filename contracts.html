Contracts & protections

Every contract behind QHOOKS on Robinhood Chain, what the code enforces, and what it doesn't.

In short: no one can change the rules, pull the liquidity or move the locked supply without 7 days' public notice.

13contracts with verified source
7 dayspublic notice before any reserve unlock
2 of 2Safe signatures to move anything

What the code enforces

  • The launch guard counts per signing wallet.It counts the wallet that signs the transaction, whichever router or app the trade goes through, and it ignores any extra data sent with the swap.
  • The opening tax falls over time.During the guard window each wallet can also only buy up to a set cap.
  • The rules are fixed at launch.The factory has no owner, no upgrade and no pause switch. Nobody can change a launch's rules after it starts.
  • The launch liquidity can't be removed.Only the trading fees it earns can be collected.
  • The reserve is time-locked.It unlocks only to the 2-of-2 QHooks Safe, and every unlock gives 7 days' public notice first.

What it doesn't do

  • Separate wallets each get their own cap.Someone with several wallets can buy up to the cap in each one, but every one of those buys pays the opening tax.
  • Transfers aren't taxed.Moving tokens from one wallet to another costs nothing.
  • The 2% tax only applies in the QHooks pool.Trades in any other pool don't pay it.
