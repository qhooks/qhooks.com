QHOOKS token.

What the token does on QHooks today and what comes next. Plans, not promises. QHOOKS token page →

  1. Live

    At launch

    • QHOOKS on Robinhood Chain.
    • Liquidity locked in the pool: fees can be collected, the liquidity can’t be pulled.
    • A 30% reserve in five on-chain locks that pay the Safe.
    • A 2% token tax: buyback and burn every 15 minutes, holder staking and referrers.
    • Holder cashback: wallets holding 100,000+ QHOOKS get 40% of the terminal fee back.
    • Staking paid in QHOOKS and WETH.
    • Listed on DexScreener.
  2. Next

    Soon after

    • Elysium mainnet.
    • Listing on GeckoTerminal.
  3. Planned

    Further out

    • Call rewards: 10% of terminal fees to callers.
    • One-tap Create.
    • Telegram login and trading in the bot.
    • Builder payments from week 3.
    • Arc and Unichain.

Phase by phase.

  1. 01Week 1Now

    Launch and build

    • The launch tools are live: qToken Studio, the terminal, the Lab and the builder marketplace.
    • Builders can start straight away: the module template, the Lab and the public API are open.
    • Builder bounties open: a community bot for launch groups and a loyalty staking module.
    • The first real launches on QHooks.
  2. 02Weeks 2–4

    First builders ship

    • Builder payments start in week 3, then every month, so entries can stack up first.
    • The first bounties close and winners are announced.
    • The first modules from outside builders, reviewed and listed.
    • QHooks pools listed with aggregators.
    • Wider use of the public API and SDK.
  3. 03Months 2–3

    Harden and widen

    • An independent security review of the contracts that hold users’ funds.
    • The next round of bounties opens.
    • More marketplace modules and Lab templates.
    • Community rewards for early users.
  4. 04Months 4–6

    Grow

    • Launches on Solana.
    • Incentive programs for creators.
    • Builder marketplace growth.
  5. 05Months 6–12

    Scale

    • Listing curation by the community.
    • A larger builder community.
