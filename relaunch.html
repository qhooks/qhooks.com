Relaunch your token on QHooks

Holders swap the old token for the new one at a fixed ratio for a set window. Old tokens are retired to the dead address, and any new tokens nobody claims are burned. Only the token's own team can relaunch it: its owner, the wallet that deployed it, or its QHooks launch creator.
