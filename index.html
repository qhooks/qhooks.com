The two-key model

One pool. Your rules. Two keys on the money.

Wallet signaturesecp256k1Key one
Post-quantum signaturehash-basedKey two

  1. 01Launch

    Name your token, pick a chain and seed the pool. Your hooks attach to it from the first block.

  2. 02Trade

    Every swap pays the LP fee you set. Your share flows into a vault only you can open.

  3. 03Graduate

    When inflow reaches your target, QGraduation moves liquidity to its next pool. Or keep the original. Your call.

  4. 04Withdraw

    Moving money takes two independent keys: your wallet and a post-quantum key. Ordinary swaps never need them.
