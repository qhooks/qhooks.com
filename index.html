Launch blocks

Pick in Studio. Fixed at launch.

  • Protection

    Anti-snipe

    A falling launch tax and a per-block buy cap keep bots off the first minutes.

    Default 25% falling to 0 over 5 min
  • Creator income

    Buy and sell tax

    A cut of every trade in the quote asset, with an optional launch rate that falls.

    Up to 10% a side, split across 8 payees
  • Rewards

    Auto burn

    A slice of every buy goes to the dead address on the spot.

    Up to 10% of each buy
  • Rewards

    Buyback and burn

    Charges fill a pot that buys the token back and burns it, inside a price limit.

    Anyone can run it after the cooldown
  • Games

    King of the pool

    The biggest buy of each round takes the pot.

    Rounds of 5 minutes to 7 days
  • Games

    Last buyer wins

    Every buy resets the clock. When it runs out, the last buyer claims the pot.

    Timers of 1 minute to 1 day
  • Fees

    Market hours

    Fees that follow US stock market sessions, daylight saving included.

    Regular, extended, overnight, weekend
  • Fees

    Storm fee

    Fees rise when the price swings, then fade back to the floor.

    Measured every minute to every day

The two-key model

One pool. Your rules. Two keys on the money.

  1. 01

    Launch

    Name your token, pick a chain and seed the pool. Your hooks attach to it from the first block.

  2. 02

    Trade

    Every swap pays the LP fee you set. Your share flows into a vault only you can open.

  3. 03

    Graduate

    When inflow reaches your target, QGraduation moves liquidity to its next pool. Or keep the original. Your call.

  4. 04

    Withdraw

    Moving money takes two independent keys: your wallet and a post-quantum key. Ordinary swaps never need them.
