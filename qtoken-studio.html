    How launch protection works
    SLH-DSA-SHA2-128s and your EVM wallet jointly authorize pool initialization, graduation-policy changes, fee claims, migration and destination withdrawals. The token remains a standard ERC20. Ordinary transfers, swaps, chain consensus and assets outside these contracts are not made quantum-protected.
    A flat platform launch fee (0.0002 ETH; 0.0007 BNB on BNB Chain) is paid once, before the first launch transaction. You fund initial liquidity and pay network fees for each transaction. Trading uses your configured LP fee plus the separately disclosed protocol surcharge. Liquidity values can fall; no yield or return is guaranteed.
    Contracts are not independently audited. Keep your encrypted key backup, its passphrase and the separate public launch-progress file. Do not send tokens directly to contract addresses to initialize liquidity.
