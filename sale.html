How this sale works

  • Half the token supply (500M) is for sale. Nobody can trade the token until the sale ends.
  • New sales start from Standard, $20K, $50K or a Custom USD cap. Caps use the full 1B supply during the sale and the remaining supply after settlement.
  • Fixed price: one price until all 500M are sold or the time runs out. If a buy would go over, the part that fits is used and the rest comes straight back.
  • Falling price: the price starts high and drops steadily to a floor. The sale ends as soon as what has been put in buys all 500M at the current price. Everyone pays the same final price, so buying early never costs more: you just get more tokens for the same amount.
  • If less than the minimum comes in by the end, the sale fails and everyone takes back exactly what they put in.
  • If it reaches the minimum, anyone can settle it in one transaction. The token is created, all the money raised plus the same value in tokens go into a Uniswap pool at the final price, and that pool position is locked forever (no one, including the creator and QHooks, can take it out). Unsold tokens are burned. Then everyone claims their tokens.
  • There is no allowlist. The creator may set a per-wallet limit.
